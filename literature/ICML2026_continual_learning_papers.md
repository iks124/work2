## Screening Method

I used the official ICML virtual-site metadata and abstracts, then searched for continual-learning-related terms. The table below is the high-precision title-based list: titles contain one or more of `continual`, `class-incremental learning`, `domain-incremental learning`, `incremental object detection`, `federated incremental learning`, `catastrophic forgetting`, `loss of plasticity`, or `lifelong`.

This is intentionally stricter than an abstract keyword search. An abstract-level search produced many false positives where terms such as `incremental`, `forgetting`, or `continual` appeared only in a general motivation or unrelated context.
